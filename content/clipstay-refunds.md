Starting with Clipstay 1.1, Pro is an optional one-time purchase of $4.99. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

## Refunds

If Clipstay Pro does not work for you, email [viktor@ermolov.dev](mailto:viktor@ermolov.dev) within 30 days of purchase and include the email address you paid with. You will get a full refund, no questions asked. Refunds go back to the original payment method; your bank may take a few business days to show them.

## What happens to Pro after a refund

Pro is turned off for that email address, so it cannot be activated on a new computer. A copy of Clipstay that was already activated keeps working as Pro, because the extension never re-checks your purchase.

If Pro is no longer active on a computer (for example, after reinstalling without activating), your saved clips are never deleted or hidden, and export stays free. Only the free plan's limits (100 unpinned clips and 5 pinned clips) apply to clips captured afterwards.

## Activation problems

If Clipstay does not recognize your purchase email, write to [viktor@ermolov.dev](mailto:viktor@ermolov.dev) with the email you paid with and the time of payment. It will be fixed by hand, and you will not be charged twice.
