faq:
  - q: "Where is my clipboard history stored?"
    a: "On your device, in Chrome's local extension storage. Uninstalling it removes the data."
  - q: "How do I keep passwords out of my history?"
    a: "Web-page capture skips password fields. The normal popup can still read text left on the shared system clipboard, including text from password managers or Incognito. Turn off “Add clipboard when the popup opens” in Settings if you do not want clipboard import."
  - q: "How do I keep a clip for good?"
    a: "Pin it. Free lets you add a pin while you have fewer than five pinned clips. In Clipstay 1.1.4, existing and imported pins are kept even above five; pinned clips do not roll off under the history limit. You can also export your history at any time."
  - q: "How do I save text from other apps?"
    a: "Leave capture active and “Add clipboard when the popup opens” enabled, copy text in the app, then open Clipstay. The popup can add the current clipboard text. Starting with Clipstay 1.1.4, unchanged text read at Resume is skipped until the popup observes different or empty clipboard content, or a new permitted copy is processed."
  - q: "What does pausing capture do?"
    a: "Starting with Clipstay 1.1.4, pausing capture pauses web-page capture and popup clipboard import. Resume reads the current clipboard and saves a separate local fingerprint before enabling capture; read or storage problems are reported. Popup import skips that unchanged text even after a browser restart. The skip ends when the popup observes different or empty clipboard content, or Clipstay processes a new permitted page copy or saved-clip copy-back. There is no timer that later imports unchanged text. Clipboard reads and capture changes are not atomic; this safeguard does not cover every text ever copied while paused or identify its origin."
  - q: "Why is the same text still skipped after I copy it again in another app?"
    a: "Starting with Clipstay 1.1.4, an identical external-app recopy cannot be distinguished from unchanged clipboard text read at Resume. First let the normal popup observe different or empty clipboard content, then copy the text again. A new permitted page copy, including a recopy of the same text, or copying a saved clip back also ends the skip."
  - q: "How do I take my history with me?"
    a: "Export it to JSON or CSV at any time and import the file on another computer in Settings. Export and import are always free. In Clipstay 1.1.4, importing keeps valid backup clips and pins even above the Free limits; it does not trim saved history to the newest 100. Later genuinely new captures can replace one oldest unpinned clip each when history is full."
  - q: "Can I undo a delete?"
    a: "Yes. After you delete a clip, press Undo or Ctrl/Cmd+Z within 5 minutes, even if you closed the popup. Undo covers the last clip you deleted."
