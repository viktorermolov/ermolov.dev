Pagestay Pro is an optional one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

- **Pagestay Pro:** $4.99.
- **Clipstay + Pagestay bundle:** $6.99, which unlocks Pro in both extensions.
- **Upgrade:** $2 for owners of Clipstay Pro or Pagestay Pro who want the other one.

## Refunds

If Pagestay Pro does not work for you, email [viktor@ermolov.dev](mailto:viktor@ermolov.dev) within 30 days of purchase and include the email address you paid with. You will get a full refund of that payment, no questions asked. Refunds go back to the original payment method; your bank may take a few business days to show them.

## What happens to Pro after a refund

A Pro license stays active as long as at least one payment that bought it has not been refunded. When a refund leaves no such payment, the purchase email can no longer be used to activate Pro for that product. This matters when you made more than one payment:

- If you bought the bundle and ask for a refund, both Pagestay Pro and Clipstay Pro end, unless another payment of yours still covers one of them.
- If you bought Clipstay Pro and then paid $2 to upgrade, refunding the $2 ends Pagestay Pro only. Clipstay Pro stays, because your first payment still covers it. The same applies the other way around.
- If you refund the original purchase but keep a later upgrade payment that also covers that product, that product stays active.

A copy of Pagestay that was already activated keeps working as Pro, because the extension never re-checks your purchase.

If Pro is no longer active on a computer (for example, after reinstalling without activating), your notes are never deleted, hidden or locked, and export and restore stay free. Only creating new Pro items, such as rules, new folders and tags or scheduled backups, needs Pro, and full-text and tag search stay Pro features.

## Activation problems

If Pagestay does not recognize your purchase email, write to [viktor@ermolov.dev](mailto:viktor@ermolov.dev) with the email you paid with and the time of payment. It will be fixed by hand, and you will not be charged twice.
